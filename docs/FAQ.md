# FAQ

## Do I Need All `mydata` Zip Files?

Yes. If Snapchat split your export into multiple `mydata` zip files, select all of them together or select the folder containing them.

## Will This Delete My Snapchat Export?

No. Original Snapchat export zips are never deleted automatically.

## Will Duplicates Be Removed Automatically?

No. The app detects exact duplicates and lets you review them first. You choose whether duplicate candidates are moved to Trash.

## Can I Upload Manually?

Yes. Use the merged EXIF ZIP option. You can manually upload that ZIP's contents wherever you want.

## Why Are Some Videos Damaged?

Some Snapchat video files are incomplete or missing video indexes. The app tries to repair videos when possible, but some files are unrecoverable and need review.

## Does Google Upload Read My Library?

The app requests the Google Photos append-only scope. It is meant to add new media, not broadly read your existing library.

## Why Does Apple Photos Reject Files?

Photos can reject unsupported, damaged, or incomplete files. The app normalizes files for Apple Photos and imports in small batches, but unrepairable files may still be rejected.

## Where Does Output Go?

Generated previews and exports are stored under:

```text
~/Documents/Snapchat Memories Export
```

## What Is Test 25?

Test 25 runs a small preview on 25 files so you can verify metadata behavior before processing a large archive.
